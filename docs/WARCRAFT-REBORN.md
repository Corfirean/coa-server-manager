# Warcraft Reborn

**English** · [Русский](ru/WARCRAFT-REBORN.md) · [Deutsch](de/WARCRAFT-REBORN.md) · [Français](fr/WARCRAFT-REBORN.md) · [Español](es/WARCRAFT-REBORN.md) · [简体中文](zh-CN/WARCRAFT-REBORN.md)

Choose **Warcraft Reborn** on Overview to create or switch to this realm. The
installed worldserver must contain Warcraft Reborn support; an old build is
refused before any databases are created. Stop the client before switching to
have its saved realm updated immediately.

Accounts are shared with your other local realms. Warcraft Reborn starts with separate characters; switching profiles does not copy existing characters or progress.

Bots are unavailable on Warcraft Reborn. Other optional modules are experimental. Portable character transfers into Warcraft Reborn are not supported. **Portable characters remain experimental.**

Backups and updates include every created realm. Check the realm recorded in a recovery point before restoring it.

Simultaneous startup supports CoA + Wildcard only. Disable **Start CoA and Wildcard together** in Settings before switching to Warcraft Reborn.
