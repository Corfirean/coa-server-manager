title: The game client now comes from the community launcher, not from the Manager
---
The community moved the game client behind its official launcher (account sign-in), so the Manager can no longer download or update it. The built-in client download and update are removed. "Set up the game client" now sends you to coa-development.org to get the launcher; once the launcher has installed the game, choose "I already have a client" and pick its folder. Linking a folder, the server address switch and the companion addon work as before.
