title: Module settings for Content Scaling include creature health
---
The Content Scaling page now has five health multipliers: for the open world, dungeons and raids, and for ordinary mobs and bosses. They multiply the health the scaling already gives creatures (1 keeps the normal balance, lower is easier, 0.1 to 10) and need a server build that includes the matching Content Scaling settings.
