title: Bot strength settings for CoA Companions
---
A "Bot Strength" section in the bot settings: switch the help for bots on or off, choose where it applies
(dungeons, raids, battlegrounds and arenas, the open world) and set how much more damage and healing bots
do, how much less damage they take and how much threat a bot tank makes. All multipliers start at 1, so
nothing changes until you raise them. Needs a CoA Companions build with bot scaling.
